What these numbers are. Page views and visitors are counted here, on this server. No third-party analytics, no advertising script, no tracking cookie. A visitor is a salted hash of an address and browser that is thrown away every night — so today's visitor count is real, and nobody can be followed from one day to the next, including by this dashboard. A user is the session id the app already keeps for credits and threads, which is why that one number can say new or returning: it is the single identifier here that outlives midnight, and it is one the site was setting anyway. Two people behind one office address are one visitor and two users; one person on a phone and a laptop is two of both. Requests come from counters the app already kept. Money comes from Paddle's own webhooks, and shows what reaches your balance after their fee, not what the customer was charged. Bots and crawlers are filtered out by user agent, which catches the honest ones and not the rest.
