What these numbers are. Page views and visitors are counted on this server — no third-party analytics or tracking cookie. A visitor is a salted hash of address and browser, thrown away nightly; a user is the session id the app already keeps, the one figure that can say new or returning. Hatched bars are days before counting began. Money comes from the payment provider's webhooks; sample events sent from its dashboard are ignored. "Monthly income, estimated" is paying subscribers times the Pro price, before the provider's fee.
