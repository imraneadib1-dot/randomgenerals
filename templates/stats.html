What these numbers are. A real person is anyone who sent at least one message — loading a page is not enough, so crawlers, link previews and people who bounced off the landing page are not in it. With an account means they were signed in; visitors typed without one. Each person is counted once per day however much they wrote, and once in "all time". Day-by-day counting began {{ r.began or 'recently' }}; "all time" also includes everyone with a conversation from before then. Everything comes from rows the app writes to do its job — nothing extra is tracked.
