# Why `.hintrc` ignores two attributes

The Edge Tools compat check flags `playsinline` and `controlslist` on
`<video>` as unsupported in Firefox and (for `controlslist`) Safari.
Both reports are correct and neither is a bug to fix.

**`playsinline` has to stay.** Without it iOS Safari refuses to play a
video inline and takes it fullscreen instead, which would break the
scroll-driven scene on every iPhone. Browsers that do not know the
attribute ignore it, so the cost of keeping it is zero and the cost of
removing it is the whole effect on mobile Safari.

**`controlslist` is a progressive nicety.** It hides the download and
remote-playback buttons on the video-bay previews in the app. Where it is
unsupported the buttons simply appear, which is the behaviour we would
have had anyway without the attribute.

Removing either to get a clean Problems panel would trade a working page
for a quiet editor. Scoping the hint says the same thing out loud.
